Tattoo Artist & Tattoo Shop on Vasna-Bhayli Road

The Kala Culture Tattoos is a tattoo studio at 102, Neelkanth Gold Complex, Vasna-Bhayli Main Road (Bhayli is also spelled Bhayali), Vadodara. Jaimin Parmar and the team do custom designs, realism, fine line, colour work, cover-ups and matching tattoos for people from Vasna, Bhayli, Atladra, Gotri, Akota, Alkapuri and the rest of Vadodara (Baroda).

Call us a tattoo artist, a tattoo shop or a tattoo studio, it is the same room and the same people. You get a free design chat first, a drawing you can change, and a price before we start.

Hygiene at our tattoo studio

Clean setup,
right in front of you.

Every appointment starts with a new needle and a freshly covered station. Ask to watch the setup. We would rather you check than wonder.

◉

Single-Use Needles

A new, sealed needle is opened for every client and thrown away after the session.

✦

Sterilised Equipment

Reusable equipment is cleaned and sterilised before it touches anyone else.

✚

Covered Station

Surfaces and machines are wrapped in barrier film and wiped down between clients.

◇

Fresh Gloves

Gloves are changed during the session whenever they need to be.

▣

A Clean, Tidy Studio

Every session starts in an organised space, so you can relax and let us work.

✓

Aftercare Advice

We wrap the tattoo and explain how to look after it while it heals.

Ask to see the setup before you sit down. It is the easiest way to judge any tattoo shop in Vadodara, ours included.
